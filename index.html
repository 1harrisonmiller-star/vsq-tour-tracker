Shows run roughly 90–120 minutes. VSQ opens with string arrangements pulled from their Bridgerton, film-score and pop catalog; on co-billed nights Girl Named Tom joins to layer their vocal harmonies over the quartet. About Vitamin String Quartet

Vitamin String Quartet is a Los Angeles classical-crossover project founded in 1999, built on string-quartet arrangements of pop, rock and hip-hop — Radiohead and Coldplay through Taylor Swift and Billie Eilish. Rather than a fixed four-piece, VSQ works as a rotating ensemble of roughly 16–20 classically trained players covering studio sessions, video work and live touring.

Their arrangements carried Netflix's Bridgerton, HBO's Westworld and Showtime's Shameless, and they've collaborated with Lana Del Rey, Björk and Sigur Rós. Seven albums have charted on Billboard's Classical and Classical Crossover charts across 300+ releases.
